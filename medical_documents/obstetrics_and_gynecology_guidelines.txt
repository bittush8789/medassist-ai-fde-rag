SECTION 1: PREECLAMPSIA WITH SEVERE FEATURES & ECLAMPSIA PROPHYLAXIS

1.1 Diagnostic Criteria for Preeclampsia with Severe Features
New-onset hypertension after 20 weeks of gestation with Systolic BP >= 160 mmHg or Diastolic BP >= 110 mmHg on two occasions at least 4 hours apart PLUS any of the following severe features:
- Thrombocytopenia (Platelets < 100,000/mcL).
- Impaired liver function (AST/ALT >= 2 times the upper limit of normal) or severe persistent right upper quadrant / epigastric pain.
- Renal insufficiency (Serum creatinine > 1.1 mg/dL or doubling of baseline).
- Pulmonary edema.
- New-onset visual disturbances (scotomata, photopsia, cortical blindness) or severe persistent headache unresponsive to analgesics.

1.2 Acute Antihypertensive Emergency Pharmacotherapy
- First-Line IV Agents (Lower BP to 140–150 / 90–100 mmHg within 30–60 minutes):
  * IV Labetalol: Initial 20 mg IV bolus over 2 min; if BP remains >= 160/110 after 10 min, give 40 mg IV, then 80 mg IV every 10 min (Max cumulative dose 220–300 mg).
  * IV Hydralazine: Initial 5–10 mg IV bolus; if unresponsive after 20 min, give 10 mg IV (Max cumulative dose 20–30 mg).
  * Oral Nifedipine Immediate-Release: 10–20 mg PO if IV access is not yet established.

1.3 Magnesium Sulfate Seizure Prophylaxis & Neuroprotection
- Dosage Protocol:
  * Loading Dose: 4 to 6 g Magnesium Sulfate IV in 100 mL IV fluid infused over 15 to 20 minutes.
  * Maintenance Infusion: 1 to 2 g/hour continuous IV infusion for at least 24 hours postpartum or after last seizure.
- Toxicity Monitoring (Check patellar reflexes, respiratory rate >= 12/min, urine output >= 30 mL/hr):
  * Antidote for Magnesium Toxicity (Loss of reflexes, respiratory arrest): Intravenous Calcium Gluconate 10% (10 mL / 1 g IV infused over 2 to 5 minutes).

SECTION 2: POSTPARTUM HEMORRHAGE (PPH) RESUSCITATION BUNDLE

2.1 Definition & Etiology
- Definition: Cumulative blood loss >= 1000 mL or blood loss accompanied by signs or symptoms of hypovolemia within 24 hours of birth.
- The 4 T's Etiology: Tone (Uterine Atony - 70%), Trauma (lacerations, hematomas), Tissue (retained placenta), Thrombin (coagulopathies).

2.2 Stepwise Uterotonic Pharmacotherapy
1. Oxytocin (First-Line): 10–40 units in 500–1000 mL IV crystalloid solution (or 10 units IM).
2. Methylergonovine (Methergine): 0.2 mg IM every 2 to 4 hours (CONTRAINDICATION: Hypertension or preeclampsia).
3. Carboprost Tromethamine (Hemabate): 250 mcg (0.25 mg) IM every 15 to 90 minutes; max 8 doses (CONTRAINDICATION: Asthma).
4. Misoprostol (Cytotec): 800 to 1000 mcg administered rectally (PR) or sublingually.
5. Tranexamic Acid (TXA): 1 g IV infused over 10 minutes within 3 hours of delivery (WOMAN Trial recommendation); repeat 1 g IV once after 30 minutes if bleeding continues.

SECTION 3: PELVIC INFLAMMATORY DISEASE (PID) TREATMENT GUIDELINES

3.1 CDC Outpatient Empiric Regimen for Acute PID
- Regimen:
  * Ceftriaxone 500 mg IM in a single dose (1 g IM for patients weighing >= 150 kg) PLUS
  * Doxycycline 100 mg PO twice daily for 14 days PLUS
  * Metronidazole 500 mg PO twice daily for 14 days (Provides essential anaerobic coverage for pelvic abscesses).
- Partner Management: All sexual partners within the preceding 60 days must be evaluated and treated empirically for Chlamydia trachomatis and Neisseria gonorrhoeae.
