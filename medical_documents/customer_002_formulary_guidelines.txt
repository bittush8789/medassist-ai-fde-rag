Section 1: Apex Clinic Oncology & Immunotherapy Guidelines
- Apex Clinic First-Line Antiemetic Regimen: Highly emetogenic chemotherapy (HEC) requires standard quadruple antiemetic prophylaxis: Oral Olanzapine 10 mg + IV Aprepitant 125 mg + IV Ondansetron 16 mg + Oral Dexamethasone 12 mg on Day 1.
- Apex Infusion Center Hypersensitivity Protocol: For all monoclonal antibody infusions (e.g. Rituximab, Trastuzumab), pre-medicate with IV Diphenhydramine 50 mg + Oral Acetaminophen 650 mg 30 minutes prior to infusion.
